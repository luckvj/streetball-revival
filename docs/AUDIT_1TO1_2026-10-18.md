# 1:1 audit, 2026-10-18

The second ranked audit of where Streetball Revival still differs from the original game (PS2, `SLUS_206.51`). The
first (`docs/AUDIT_1TO1_2026-10-16.md`) set a fix order whose items 1 to 7 all have passes merged; section 0 below
says which of its items are fully closed. This one ranks what is left by how much a player sees it, then by how much
evidence a fix already has, and ends with a fix order of session-sized pieces, one heavy session at a time.

State audited: `main` at dc5687d (1,135 workspace tests, snapshot VERSION 52; scorecard 138 obligations, 98 matched,
16 known differences, 24 untested, 1 deferred). Every item names its evidence: "measured" means numbers from the
original's own captures under `$EVIDENCE`; "labelled" means the rewrite's code marks it as its own or a stand-in;
"static" means read from the executable. For each item, **Fix needs** says what evidence a fix needs: a static trace
only, an existing capture, or a new capture.

How this was done:

- The three attract captures (`street-native-ai-attract-1/2/3.jsonl`, computer against computer) through
  `crates/street-sim/src/ai/capture.rs` on main, 17 of 17 tests passing on each: 16-seed means of games of each
  capture's length on each capture's own players (the first's inferred Lakers against Portland, the third's recorded
  Indiana against San Antonio; the second keeps every player at 50 and the disc's team pairs), plus the reports'
  one-game breakdowns.

  `crates/street-sim/src/played_capture.rs`, 25 of 25 tests passing, with their reports; and a scratch script over
  the played captures' controller words for who the person controls.
- Capture 3's turbo bars for what a computer's dunk costs.
- Every "the rewrite's own", "stand-in", "not ported", "untraced" and "not traced" label: 142 in `crates/`, 103 in
  `docs/TRACE.md`; the `tuning` module's placeholder constants by whether a local match still runs them.
- The scorecard's 41 open cases, the handoff's open lists, each pass's TRACE section's "left" list,
  `docs/FRONTEND_RECOVERY.md` and the front end's page styles, the online setup.

## 1. What a person playing feels first

### 1.1 Who a person's pass goes to

`World::pass_button` (`passing.rs`) picks the receiver best lined up with the stick, or with the passer's facing
when the stick is at rest, "the rewrite's own pick"; only the leaper of an alley-oop is the original's
(`FUN_00146278`, `0x146860..0x1468c8`). The trick passes for a person are the rewrite's conditions: Off the Heezay

rewrite's own (the original's passout special, game+0x160, is not traced). The give 'n go on the right stick and the
passer keeping control are `controls.rs`'s own. Every pass a person throws goes through this.

Evidence: labelled. The played captures hold 7 of the person's passes, all to the receiver the rewrite also picks,
too few to show the rule. **Fix needs:** a static trace of `FUN_00146278` whole (the target, the head and glass
branches, the passout special) and a new played capture of the person passing with the stick in each direction, at
rest, and with two turbos near a defender and on a drive.

**Done 2026-10-18 (branch `presses`, docs/TRACE.md "The person's presses").** `FUN_00146278` is ported whole
