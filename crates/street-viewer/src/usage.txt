usage: street-viewer [--iso <path>] [--court <ID>] [--list]
                     [--view <yaw>,<pitch>,<distance>] [--focus <x>,<y>,<z>]
                     [--screenshot <out.png>] [--hide <model>,...]
                     [--player <n>] [--home] [--body <model>] [--head <model>]
                     [--anim <name>] [--hires <dir>]

  --iso         the original game (USA) disc image. Default: $STREET_ISO, or
                "the original game (USA).iso" in this folder or a parent.
  --court       court archive ID, e.g. RUCKER, PHILLY, NYCAGE (see --list). Default RUCKER.
  --list        print the court IDs on the disc and exit.
  --view        starting camera: yaw and pitch in degrees, distance in game units.
  --focus       point the camera orbits, x,y,z in game units. Default 0,0,0.
  --screenshot  save one frame to this PNG and exit.
  --hide        leave out these models, e.g. ruckerb,ruckerd (names as printed at startup).
  --player      stand database player n at centre court, in the rest pose:
                0-144 league players, 145-157 streetballers, 158-183 legends,
                184-233 generic ballers (list them with the db_report example).
  --home        dress the player in the team's white home uniform.
  --body        a create-a-player kit body instead, e.g. body0_outfit6, dressed
                in the --player's textures.
  --head        a create-a-player kit head. Default malehair02_head00_body0.
  --anim        loop this animation on the player, e.g. standrest or createplayer_stand
                (list them with: cargo run -p street-assets --example anim_report -- <iso> <filter>).
  --hires       a Dolphin texture pack for the GameCube Vol. 2 (a Textures/GNZE69 folder,
                e.g. the Legendary Edition's): its HD faces, jerseys, shoes and logos
                stand in for the disc's images.

mouse: drag to orbit, wheel to zoom.  keys: WASD/arrows move, Q/E down/up, Esc quits.
